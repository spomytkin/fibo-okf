# jurisdiction of Colombia

Canonical resource: <https://spec.edmcouncil.org/fibo/ontology/BE/GovernmentEntities/LatinAmericanJurisdiction/SouthAmericanGovernmentEntitiesAndJurisdictions/JurisdictionOfColombia>
