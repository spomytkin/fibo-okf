# Government of the Holy See

Canonical resource: <https://spec.edmcouncil.org/fibo/ontology/BE/GovernmentEntities/EuropeanJurisdiction/SouthernEuropeGovernmentEntitiesAndJurisdictions/GovernmentOfTheHolySee>

## Definition
