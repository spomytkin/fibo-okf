# United Kingdom jurisdiction

Canonical resource: <https://spec.edmcouncil.org/fibo/ontology/BE/GovernmentEntities/EuropeanJurisdiction/UKGovernmentEntitiesAndJurisdictions/UnitedKingdomJurisdiction>
