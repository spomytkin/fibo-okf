# European Economic Area jurisdiction

Canonical resource: <https://spec.edmcouncil.org/fibo/ontology/BE/GovernmentEntities/EuropeanJurisdiction/EUGovernmentEntitiesAndJurisdictions/EuropeanEconomicArea>

## Definition
