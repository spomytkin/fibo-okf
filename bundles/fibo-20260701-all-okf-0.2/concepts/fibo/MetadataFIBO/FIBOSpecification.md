- **abstract**: The Financial Industry Business Ontology (FIBO) is a collaborative effort among industry practitioners, semantic technology experts and information scientists to standardize the language used to precisely define the terms, conditions, and characteristics of financial instruments; the legal and relationship structure of business entities; the content and time dimensions of market data; and the legal obligations and process aspects of corporate actions. The definitions and relationships that comprise FIBO have been modularized into a number of domains, which in turn include a number of modules, each of which is further modularized into one or more constituent ontologies.
