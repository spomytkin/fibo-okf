## Annotations

- **label**: business objective
- **definition**: objective that reflects the strategic goals and direction of a business within a time frame and available resources
- **explanatoryNote**: Business objectives allow an organization to define its goals and direction. A company uses strategy and tactics at every level of its operation to achieve its objectives.
