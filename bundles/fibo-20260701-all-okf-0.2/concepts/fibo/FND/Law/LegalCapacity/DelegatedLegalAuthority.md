# delegated legal authority

Canonical resource: <https://spec.edmcouncil.org/fibo/ontology/FND/Law/LegalCapacity/DelegatedLegalAuthority>

## Definition

institutionalized and legal power inherent in a particular job, function, or position that is meant to enable its holder to successfully carry out his or her responsibilities, where such power has been delegated through some formal means

## Relationships

- **Subclass of**: [LegalCapacity](/concepts/fibo/FND/Law/LegalCapacity/LegalCapacity.md)

## Annotations

- **label**: delegated legal authority
- **definition**: institutionalized and legal power inherent in a particular job, function, or position that is meant to enable its holder to successfully carry out his or her responsibilities, where such power has been delegated through some formal means
- **scopeNote**: This specifically means the authority to make legally binding commitments.
