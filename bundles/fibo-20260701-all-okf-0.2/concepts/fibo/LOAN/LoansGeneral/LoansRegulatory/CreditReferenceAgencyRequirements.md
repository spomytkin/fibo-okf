# credit reference agency requirements

Canonical resource: <https://spec.edmcouncil.org/fibo/ontology/LOAN/LoansGeneral/LoansRegulatory/CreditReferenceAgencyRequirements>

## Definition

REquirements other than data protection, governing what a credit reference agency can or cannot do.

## Annotations

- **label** (en): credit reference agency requirements
- **definition** (en): REquirements other than data protection, governing what a credit reference agency can or cannot do.
- **editorialNote** (en): Example might include legislation about whether a consumer can be given a bad rating for no good reason; must presumably be some audit requirements about how ratings are arrived at (llogically - no one knows of this; may be addressed simply by the indivbidual's right to see what's said.
