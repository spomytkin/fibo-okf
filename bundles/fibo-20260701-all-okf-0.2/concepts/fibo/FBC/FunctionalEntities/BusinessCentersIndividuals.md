## Annotations

- **abstract**: This ontology includes individuals representing the set of international business centers corresponding to those identified in FpML as well as additional municipalities called out in the ISO 10383 Codes for exchanges and market identification (MIC) standard. 		This set of business centers is current with respect to the FpML published XML data as of Q2 2023 and additional municipalities included in the MIC codes as of Q1 2026. Note that we have deviated from the standard FIBO naming convention of strict use of camel case to add underscores in certain city names for readability purposes.
